# Code of Conduct

Clark follows the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 2.1.

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and direct.
- Discuss technical disagreements with evidence.
- Keep feedback focused on the work.
- Protect user privacy and project credentials.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Sharing credentials, private memory records, or sensitive logs.
- Deliberately misleading users about whether a feature works with real integrations.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or ban participants for behavior that harms the project or its users.
